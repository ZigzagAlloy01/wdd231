Membership benefits

NP Membership

Designed for non-profit organizations that want to connect with the local business community.

  • Basic organization listing in the Chamber directory
  • Access to selected community networking events
  • Chamber newsletters and local business updates
  • Invitations to non-profit collaboration opportunities

Membership benefits

Bronze Membership

A practical option for small and emerging businesses building their local presence.

  • Standard business listing in the Chamber directory
  • Access to member networking events
  • Discounted registration for selected workshops
  • Monthly business newsletter and resource updates

Membership benefits

Silver Membership

For established organizations seeking more visibility, learning, and engagement.

  • Enhanced business directory listing
  • All Bronze benefits
  • Priority consideration for a Chamber website spotlight
  • Two complimentary seats at selected training sessions
  • Discounted event and luncheon registration

Membership benefits

Gold Membership

A premier option for organizations looking for expanded promotion and deeper participation.

  • Premium business directory placement
  • All Silver benefits
  • Priority business spotlight opportunities
  • Complimentary seats at selected Chamber events
  • Enhanced event sponsorship and advertising opportunities
  • Priority invitations to leadership and business roundtables
